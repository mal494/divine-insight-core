# Catalog

Three tiers, cheapest to build first.

## Digital goods

No inventory, no shipping, highest margin. Build these first.

1. **Printable deck PDF** - all 78 card faces laid out for home printing
2. **Guidebook PDF** - every card's upright and reversed meaning, generated
   directly from Core so it can never drift from the apps
3. **Spread sheets** - printable layouts for the spreads taught in Academy
4. **Journal pages** - printable reading-log pages matching the app journal

The guidebook is the important one: it is a build step over Core, not a writing
project.

## Print on demand

No upfront inventory. A supplier prints and ships per order.

1. Art prints of individual Major Arcana cards
2. Poster of the full 78-card deck
3. Apparel with card imagery

Blocked on artwork: Web currently renders glyph placeholders for most cards
(`src/data/artwork.ts` in mal494/DI). Art is the real dependency here, not code.

## Physical deck

The flagship, and the only item needing real capital.

1. 78-card printed deck, custom box
2. Printed guidebook bundled with it

Needs: complete card art, a print run quote, and a decision on whether to
pre-sell or fund it outright.

## Sequencing

Digital goods ship first and fund the art. Art unlocks print on demand. Print on
demand proves demand for the physical deck. Do not start at the deck.
